### You

You are a copywriter for Wildframe, a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. 
Our brand is warm, plainspoken, and a little irreverent, with no corporate jargon.
Our customers are environmentally conscious urban professionals.
Write 6 perk ideas for subscribers that stay six months or longer. Return a numbered list, each one sentence with no more than 25 words. 

### Claude

1. Free bag of our rarest single-origin beans, shipped the moment you hit your six-month anniversary with us.

2. Lock in your price forever—no rate hikes, ever, even when coffee costs climb for everyone else.

3. Skip the line with early access to limited harvests before we announce them to the rest of the world.

4. Swap bags for free, unlimited times, if you ever want to chase a different flavor without extra fees.

5. Get a reusable canvas tote and steel tumbler, because your loyalty deserves less plastic, not more stuff.

6. Priority invites to virtual cupping sessions with our roaster, where you taste test beans before they launch.

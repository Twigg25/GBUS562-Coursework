### You

You are writing coffee bean cards for Wildframe, a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. 
Our brand is warm, plainspoken, and a little irreverent, with no corporate jargon.
Here is one finished card. Match its layout exactly: the same five lines, labels, and order.
HUILA, COLOMBIA
Roast: Medium
Tastes like: red apple, brown sugar, cocoa
Brew it: Pour-over or drip
Real talk: The coffee that converted our office's tea drinker. Mostly.
Write a card in this exact layout for each of these coffees:
•	Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia. Light roast. Notes: jasmine, lemon, bergamot. Best as pour-over.
•	Cerrado, Brazil. Medium-dark roast. Notes: peanut, milk chocolate, low acidity. Good for espresso or French press.
•	Chiapas, Mexico. Medium roast. Notes: almond, caramel, orange peel. Good for drip or AeroPress.
Return only the three completed bean cards, each with exactly five lines and the labels in the same order as the example. Separate cards with one blank line. Do not add any introductory text or explanations.
